Fueling

What the crew logs at each aid station, and the hourly goal for each. These are the columns in the leg log and the lines on the intake charts. Most races want calories, fluid and sodium. Add carbs or caffeine if you track them, remove any you do not.

Name Unit Short Goal / hr
Fills the goals above, the notes and the hour bands below, from a runner's saved profile. These start as everyone's on this race; once it exists, the pit board sets them per runner. Everything stays editable before you create the race.
Notes for the crew (optional)

What the runner wants the crew to know without being asked. Allergies, what settles late, what to do when they go quiet. Loading their plan brings these across from their profile. Every runner starts on these; the pit board sets them per runner afterwards.

Hour bands (optional)

When the goals should change as the race wears on. A band overrides only the numbers you fill in. Bands are hours from the start, and the clock time each begins is shown beside it, because what usually changes is the weather rather than the runner.

Shorthand (optional)

The things you hand over by the item, not by the number. One tap on the pit form adds a serving, two taps adds two, and the crew counts flasks instead of doing arithmetic at an aid station. You can add these later too.

Course GPX (optional)

Drives the course map, the elevation profile, and each segment's climb.
