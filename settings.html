Notes for the crew

What the racer wants the crew to know without being asked. Allergies, what settles late, what to do when they go quiet. Loading their goals brings these across from their profile; edit them here for this race.

Hour bands

When the goals above should change as the race wears on. A band overrides only the numbers you fill in; the rest stay on the goals above. Leave this empty and those goals hold the whole way.
Bands are hours from the start, and the clock time each one begins is shown beside it, because what changes is usually the weather rather than the racer.

Shorthand

The things you hand over by the item, not by the number. One tap on the pit form adds a serving, two taps adds two, and the crew counts flasks instead of doing arithmetic at an aid station. A flask is a flask whoever drinks it, so these are the race's rather than one racer's.
