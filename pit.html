Hour bands

When the goals above should change as the race wears on. A band overrides only the numbers you fill in; the rest stay on the goals above. Leave this empty and those goals hold the whole way.
Bands are hours from the start, and the clock time each one begins is shown beside it, because what changes is usually the weather rather than the runner.
