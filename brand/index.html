Colour

One hue, two values. A colour reads heavier on a light ground and lighter on a dark one, so each ground gets the value that keeps the mark's perceived weight constant. They are not interchangeable, and neither is a substitute for the other in code.

  • The signal is the only saturated brand colour on a page. The race states are what change during a race; if the chrome competes with them, the page stops being readable at an aid station.
  • Beam is a glow, never a surface. At 1.3:1 against white it can't sit behind text. Hover states, focus rings, the one live figure.
  • Never swap the two signal values. #0FB8BF on cream is too light to hold; #0A8B92 on void goes muddy.

Type

Three faces, one job each. The condensed display face is the language of race bibs, course signage and timing boards — it belongs to the sport rather than to a template. The wordmark's letterforms are outlines, so the logo is independent of all three.

Voice

The app is read one-handed, outdoors, by someone who has been awake since four in the morning. That is the whole brief.

Sounds like SendOff
  • “Send Off · Kaymoor Trailhead → Cunard River Access”
  • “Estimated split — watch lost signal near mile 35.”
  • “On pace · 14 min to cutoff”
  • “Anyone can find it from the hub and view live without an account.”
Doesn't
  • “Oops! Something went wrong 😅”
  • “Unleash your ultra potential.”
  • “Sync webhook configuration”
  • “We're sorry, but we were unable to process your request at this time.”
  • Name the thing the crew names. Aid stations, legs, splits, pits — their words, not the schema's.
  • A control says what happens, then says it happened. “Send Off”, then the leg starts and the timer runs.
  • Say the uncertainty. Estimated splits are labelled estimated. Elevation says est. Crews make decisions on these numbers.
  • No exclamation marks, no apologies, no emoji. An error explains what broke and what to do.

Misuse

The lockup is one piece of artwork with two colour variables. Everything below breaks something that the rest of this page depends on — the perceived weight, the geometry, or the contrast that keeps it readable at an aid station.

Don't stretch or condenseThe 16° lean and the stroke weight are fixed. Scale it with --so-wm-size, which only ever scales both axes together.
Don't recolour itTwo values of one hue, and the signal is the only saturated brand colour. The warm accent belongs to cutoffs, not to the logo.
Don't put the dark-ground pair on a light groundCream letters on cream. Set .so-on-light and the component picks the right pair itself.
…or the light-ground pair on a dark one#0A8B92 goes muddy on void and the near-black letters disappear. The two are never interchangeable.
Don't set it on a busy groundThe pulse line is a 62px stroke at full size — pattern behind it reads as more pulse. Flat grounds only.
Don't drop it on an arbitrary colourUse the grounds above. Anything else is untested for contrast and usually fails the mark before it fails the letters.
Don't rotate or re-skew itThe lean is already in the artwork. Adding rotation reads as a second, competing angle.
Don't take the lockup below 24pxThe pulse closes up and the counter fills in. Below the floor, use the mark alone.

Tokens

tokens.css is the source of truth and prefixes everything with --so-. lib/race-theme.css carries the same values unprefixed (--signal, --beam, --text) for the app's own components — when you are writing app CSS, reach for the unprefixed name.

TokenValueWhere it is used
--so-signal-on-dark#0FB8BFWordmark, controls and status chrome on dark
--so-signal-on-light#0A8B92Wordmark and CTAs on cream / white
--so-beam#5EFDF6Glow only — hover, focus rings, the one live figure
--so-ink-on-dark#F0ECE3Letterforms and body copy on dark
--so-wm-letter-on-light#0D1117Letterforms on light, as drawn
--so-ink-on-light#121A1FBody copy on light — a half-step warmer than the letters
--so-void#0A0F14App ground
--so-panel#121820Raised surface
--so-line#253340Hairlines on dark
--so-cream#F5F2EALight ground — print, marketing
--so-deep#0A5F68Solid teal surface
--so-icon-tile#0D1117Favicon / app-icon tile, as drawn
--so-wm-size30px in headersSets lockup height; width follows at 2.81:1

The two ground presets — .so-on-dark and .so-on-light — set --so-ink and --so-signal for whatever sits inside them, so the wordmark component picks up the right pair without being told which ground it is on.
