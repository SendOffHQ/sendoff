# Captions for the feature posts

One caption per `sendoff-feature-*.png`, in posting order. The first line of
each is the hook, the part Instagram shows before "more". No em dashes, to
match the site's copy.

Keep these true. Each claim here was checked against what ships; if a feature
changes, change its caption in the same commit.

---

## 1. Live race page (`sendoff-feature-live-race.png`)

Where are they now? Right here. 📍

Every split your crew logs shows up on the race page within seconds. Pace against the cutoff, projected finish, and which leg they're on right now. Family and friends just open the link. No app, no account, no refreshing.

This is Sangre de Cristo 100, 11 hours in and on pace.

Free in beta, link in bio.

#ultrarunning #ultramarathon #trailrunning #100miler #crewlife #sendoff

## 2. Course map (`sendoff-feature-map.png`)

Every climb, on the map. ⛰️

The race page shows the whole course with every aid station marked, an estimate of where your racer is right now, and the elevation profile underneath. When they go quiet for three hours, you can see the reason is a 2,000 ft climb.

Link in bio.

There's a camera button too, because nobody has the signal to browse a gallery at mile 60.

Real photos from Sangre de Cristo 100, start line to finish.

## 5. Pit board (`sendoff-feature-pit.png`)

Know when to have the chair ready. 🪑

The pit board is the crew's screen. It shows how long they've been out, where they're headed, and one big button when they arrive. Hit Send Off when they leave. Log fuel and notes for the leg while you wait for the next one.

Built for crew with full hands and a cold coffee.

#crewlife #ultracrew #ultrarunning #aidstation #100miler #sendoff

## 6. Fuel plan (`sendoff-feature-fuel-plan.png`)

Hour 30 is not hour 3. 🍜

Set fuel targets that change as the race goes on: the first night, the afternoon heat, day two when solid food stops working. The racer screen and race page always track against whichever band is live.

These are the real bands from Sangre de Cristo 100.

#ultranutrition #ultrarunning #fuelingstrategy #100miler #trailrunning #sendoff

## 7. No signal (`sendoff-feature-offline.png`)

No bars? No problem. 📵

Every tap is saved on the phone first and sends itself the moment you have signal again. No lost splits, no retyping, no standing on a picnic table waving your phone at the sky.

Built for canyons, ridgelines and aid stations in the middle of nowhere.

#trailrunning #ultrarunning #backcountry #crewlife #100miler #sendoff

## 9. One-tap items (`sendoff-feature-reorder.png`)

Log your gel without breaking stride. ⚡

The racer screen turns everything you carry into a button: gels, salt, flasks, ibuprofen. One tap logs one. Tap Reorder and drag the ones you use most to the top. Mis-tapped? Take it back with one more tap.

Made after racing Sangre de Cristo 100 self-crewed.

#ultrarunning #selfsupported #trailrunning #ultranutrition #100miler #sendoff

## 10. Write-in notes (`sendoff-feature-write-in.png`)

Quesadilla at mile 60? 🌮

Aid stations never have what you planned for. The write-in box handles everything that isn't a button: what you ate, a sock change, taped a hot spot. Type it, hit Log, keep moving.

#ultrarunning #aidstationfood #trailrunning #selfsupported #100miler #sendoff

## 11. Hourly intake (`sendoff-feature-per-hour.png`)

Eat to the plan, hour by hour. 📊

The racer screen shows calories, fluid and sodium for the current hour against your target. Green means you're on it. The other colors mean it's time to eat something.

Your stomach keeps score. Now you can too.

#ultranutrition #ultrarunning #sodium #hydration #100miler #sendoff

## 12. Finish card (`sendoff-feature-finish-card.png`)

You earned it. Post it. 🏁

When the race is done, SendOff makes you a finish card: your time, the course profile and the numbers that matter. It comes in four sizes (4:5, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9) and you choose what goes on it. It's made on your phone, and nothing gets uploaded.

35:41:15 at Sangre de Cristo 100. 🏔️

#ultrafinisher #100milefinisher #ultrarunning #trailrunning #finishline #sendoff

## 18. Private races (`sendoff-feature-private.png`)

Some races are just for the crew. 🔒

Make a race private and it stays off the race list and out of search. Only the people you invite can open it. It's good for FKT attempts, training runs and anything you're not ready to announce.

Private races will be part of Pro. Sign up during the beta and you keep them.

Decide before race day: you can take a public race private later, but anything shared while it was public stays out there.
